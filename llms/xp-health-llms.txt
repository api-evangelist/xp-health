# XP Health

> XP Health is an AI-powered vision benefits platform for employers, offering full-replacement and dual-option vision programs that reduce employee out-of-pocket eyewear costs. The platform serves 6,300+ organizations with in-app prescription renewals, virtual try-on across 5,000+ eyewear styles, and a network reaching 90% of the U.S. population. Backed by QED Investors. XP Health does not currently publish a public developer portal or API.
